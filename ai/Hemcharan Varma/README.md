## Summary of AI Usage

AI tools were used as supplementary assistance throughout the development of this project. They supported the learning, implementation, experimentation, and analysis processes, while the project team remained responsible for carrying out and verifying the implementation, experiments, and final results.

The main areas where AI assistance was used include:

1. **Setup and Configuration:** Guidance on configuring the experimental environment, setting up the machines, establishing network connectivity, and troubleshooting setup-related issues.
2. **Code Development and Debugging:** Assistance with writing, understanding, debugging, and improving the implementation of different I/O multiplexing mechanisms.
3. **Benchmarking:** Guidance on designing and executing benchmarking experiments, configuring benchmarking tools, and troubleshooting experimental scripts.
4. **Results and Analysis:** Assistance with interpreting benchmarking results, understanding performance differences, analyzing throughput, latency, resource utilization, and system calls, and identifying possible bottlenecks.
5. **Technical Understanding and Documentation:** Clarifying underlying concepts, understanding trade-offs between different I/O mechanisms, and refining technical explanations and documentation.
